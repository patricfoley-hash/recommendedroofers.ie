Fascia & Soffit

Fascia & Soffit Problems We Fix

Damaged fascia and soffit affects more than just appearances — it can let water and pests into your roof structure

Rotten Timber Fascia

Old timber fascia boards rot over time, allowing water and pests into your roof structure. Replacement is essential.

Cracked uPVC

Storm damage and age cause uPVC fascia and soffit to crack. We replace individual boards or full runs.

Pest Entry Points

Gaps in soffits allow birds, bats and insects to nest in your roof. We seal and replace to close entry points.

Water Ingress

Damaged fascia allows rainwater to run behind the gutters and into the roof structure, causing serious damp damage.

Poor Appearance

Peeling, stained or mismatched fascia and soffit dramatically reduces your home's kerb appeal.

Ventilation Issues

Old solid soffits block ventilation. We fit ventilated uPVC soffit to maintain healthy airflow in your roof.

Fascia & Soffit Questions

The fascia is the vertical board at the edge of your roof that the gutters are attached to. The soffit is the horizontal board underneath, between the fascia and the wall. Both need to be in good condition to protect your roof.
