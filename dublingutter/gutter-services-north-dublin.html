What Gutters Are Like in North Dublin

Two things shape gutter work on the northside. The coast — from Clontarf round to Malahide and Portmarnock — brings salt air that corrodes brackets and fixings far faster than inland, so we use marine-grade fixings as standard on those jobs. Inland, the great 1930s to 1970s housing schemes were built at the same time and are now reaching the end of their gutter life together, which is why we do so many full replacements across Cabra, Crumlin's northside equivalents, Artane and Coolock.
