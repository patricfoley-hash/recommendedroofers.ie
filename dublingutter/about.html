Our Story

Dublin's Gutter Experts Since 1994

Recommended Roofing & Guttering was founded over 30 years ago with one goal: to provide Dublin homeowners and businesses with a reliable, professional and fairly priced guttering service they could always count on.

What started as a small local operation has grown into one of Dublin's most trusted gutter companies — built almost entirely on word-of-mouth referrals and repeat customers. That tells you everything you need to know about the quality of our work.

We're proud of our reputation. Every job we take on, whether it's a simple gutter clean or a full fascia and soffit replacement, gets the same level of care and professionalism.

Our Values

What Makes Us Different

The values that have shaped our business since day one

Honesty First

We always tell you what the job needs — even if that means recommending a cheaper option. Your trust matters more than a bigger invoice.

On Time, Every Time

We show up when we say we will. We know your time is valuable and we respect that by being punctual and efficient on every job.

Quality Materials

We only use the highest quality PVC and aluminium products. Your gutters will be fitted to last — not just to look good on the day.

Leave No Mess

We treat your property with respect. Our team cleans up fully after every job — you won't even know we were there, except your gutters will be working perfectly.

Fully Insured

All our work is fully insured and guaranteed. You have complete peace of mind that any issues will be resolved promptly at no extra cost.
