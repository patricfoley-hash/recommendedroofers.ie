Downpipes

Downpipe Problems We Fix

Downpipe issues cause wall damage faster than most homeowners realise — get them fixed before the damage spreads

Cracked Downpipes

Cracks cause water to spray against your wall and seep into brickwork, causing damp and staining.

Blocked Downpipes

Leaves and debris compact inside downpipes over time. We clear all blockages and flush the full run.

Detached Downpipes

Downpipes that have pulled away from the wall or disconnected from the gutter outlet need immediate reattachment.

Broken Clips & Brackets

Downpipe clips and brackets fail over time. We replace all securing hardware to hold pipes firmly to the wall.

Overflow at Gutter Outlet

If the fall pipe has detached from the gutter outlet, water pours freely down the wall. We resecure and seal.

Wrong Pipe Size

Undersized downpipes can't handle heavy rainfall. We assess and upgrade to the correct pipe diameter.

Our Service

Our Downpipe Repair & Replacement Service

Downpipes are often overlooked until they fail — and by then, the water damage to walls and foundations can be significant. We carry a full range of downpipe parts on our vans and can fix most problems in a single visit.

Where a downpipe is beyond repair, we replace the full section or run with a matching or upgraded pipe in PVC or aluminium. We'll also check the gutter outlet and drain connection while we're there.

  • All downpipe sizes and profiles repaired
  • PVC and aluminium replacements available
  • Blockages cleared with pressure flush
  • Clips and brackets replaced as needed
  • Gutter outlet and drain connection checked
  • Black, white and brown finishes available
